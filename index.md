# Welcome!

I am a first-year PhD student at the University of Lille, supervised by <a href="https://sorcej.github.io/Jenny.G.Sorce/">Jenny Sorce</a> (cosmologist at CNRS) and <a href="https://pthouvenin.github.io/">Pierre-Antoine Thouvenin</a> (associate professor at Centrale Lille). I work in the <b>C</b>entre de <b>R</b>echerche en <b>I</b>nformatique, <b>S</b>ignal e<b>t</b> <b>A</b>utomatique de <b>L</b>ille (<a href="https://www.cristal.univ-lille.fr/">CRIStAL</a>) laboratory as part of the <a href="https://www.cristal.univ-lille.fr/equipes/sigma/">SigMA team</a>. My PhD project is funded by the ANR Chaire WILL <a href="https://sorcej.github.io/Jenny.G.Sorce/universitwins.html">UNIVERSITWINS</a> hold by Jenny Sorce and <a href="https://homepages.usm.uni-muenchen.de/dolag/">Klaus Dolag</a> (computational astrophysicist at LMU Munich).

My research focuses on solving inverse problems applied in cosmology, especially on galaxy clusters, through Bayesian inference.

<b>PhD thesis title:</b>  Bayesian Estimate of Galaxy Clusters for Improved Cosmological Parameter Inference.
